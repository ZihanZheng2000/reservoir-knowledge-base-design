## User Review Notes

The current inclusion rule may be slightly too strict if the goal is to build a broader reservoir operation knowledge base rather than only a narrow operating-rule collection.

Sources can be useful even when they are not direct operating manuals, if they are clearly connected to Lake Powell / Glen Canyon Dam or to upstream/downstream Colorado River conditions affected by Glen Canyon Dam operations.

Potentially useful broader evidence includes:

- downstream environmental effects;
- temperature and ecology;
- sediment and sandbar response;
- hydropower impacts;
- stakeholder concerns;
- policy and governance debates;
- media/context sources that identify operational controversies or infrastructure risks.

## Revised Working Inclusion Principle

Do not include sources only because they generally mention the Colorado River. However, include sources when they are directly connected to Lake Powell / Glen Canyon Dam, or to upstream/downstream conditions substantially shaped by Glen Canyon Dam operations, and when they can support:

- operational KUs;
- impact KUs;
- governance KUs;
- stakeholder/context KUs;
- source verification;
- cross-document synthesis.
