# Legacy Retrieval-Indexing Path

The workflow no longer treats retrieval as a knowledge-base construction stage.
Use `skills/indexing/` to encode validated Evidence Units, Knowledge Cards, and
Synthesis Cards into index-ready records. Retrieval and retrieval evaluation
are outside this construction skill and will be handled in validation work.
