# Transfer Validation Candidate Decision

Status: candidate selected for feasibility screening; transfer run not started.

## Recommended candidate

Use **Lake Oroville** as the first transfer-validation candidate, subject to a
short source-availability screen. It offers a useful contrast with the Lake
Powell development case while remaining within a well-documented U.S. reservoir
setting. The intended contrasts are a different operating authority and
governance context, strong flood/safety and water-supply roles, and a different
mix of operating manuals, public safety material, hydrologic products, and state
documentation.

This is a methodological choice, not yet an evidence claim about corpus
adequacy. Before freezing the transfer case, run only a feasibility screen and
confirm that the following minimum source families are accessible:

1. authoritative operating or water-control rules;
2. infrastructure and safety documentation;
3. current or historical operating/data products;
4. governance or environmental decision documents;
5. independent technical/research evidence.

## Acceptance rule

Accept Lake Oroville when the feasibility screen finds at least one usable,
KU-ready source in each of the five families and at least two high-density core
documents with stable locators. Otherwise record the failed screen and assess
the next candidate; do not weaken the rule after seeing extraction results.

## Frozen transfer constraints

- Keep the five workflow stages and current KU/synthesis taxonomies.
- Keep the validation protocol and reviewer scales unchanged.
- Permit reservoir names, authority names, and search terms only as recorded
  configuration.
- Do not tune prompts against Lake Oroville outputs before the first transfer
  scores are frozen.
- Treat any later workflow revision as a new transfer-validation version.
